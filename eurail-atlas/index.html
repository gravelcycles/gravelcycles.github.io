        COVERAGE & SOURCES

        Explore the country coverage and source register for this dated snapshot.

        Timetable data: opentransportdata.swiss

        This atlas represents scheduled services for 1–7 November 2026. Frequencies count trains and confirmed temporary replacement buses together as services; they do not measure passenger use or prove actual running. Temporary replacements are marked and can be hidden in Filters. Frequency counts services between recorded station calls. Overlapping express and local paths are not summed into a single physical-track total. Selecting a line adds an outline and keeps its frequency color. Where strokes overlap, clicking selects the visible stroke. Frequency includes recorded same-service journeys through intermediate calls. Traffic estimates train traversals on shared railway sections. Hubs follows retained railway paths for direct services between selected major cities, leaving unsupported geometry as gaps and counting each route separately; Countries frames the available network in one country.

        National and regional source feeds can overlap. Source filters retain that distinction; these are not certified counts of unique trains across Europe.

        The Eurail views show canonical included services plus a small, separately labelled set reviewed against current primary sources for Global Pass second class. The optional amber overlay shows conditional and unknown pass eligibility, separately from confirmed included counts. Known exclusions stay excluded. Eurail fee checkboxes can match any selected condition or require all of them, counting each included service once. All and None select every fee category or no fee category; No extras only requires explicit evidence of no mandatory reservation and no listed train supplement. Fees unresolved never qualify as free. Turn off confirmed inclusion and enable uncertain coverage to inspect only unknown or conditional pass eligibility. “No extra charge required” means no mandatory reservation or listed train supplement; optional reservations, upgrades and accommodation may cost extra. Unknown fees never qualify as free. Seat availability is not inferred.

        Railway alignments are source-provided or inferred from physical infrastructure. Recovered paths are solid and have an independent “Inferred rail paths” switch. They follow the shortest connected known railway path available between recorded stops, which may differ from the train’s operating route. They do not certify exact operating track. Known services with unresolved geometry use dashed links between recorded stops. These lines can cut across land or water: they do not trace the tracks used. Click a line for an explanation, or uncheck “Approximate paths · dotted” in Filters. Choose “Inferred only” to inspect recovered railway paths, or turn off “Inferred rail paths” to hide just those paths. Choose “Dotted only” to inspect the remaining approximate links. Connections with missing or invalid locations are withheld from the map; their source timetable records are retained.

        Filters provides independent Trains, Replacement buses, Affected railways and City labels controls. Each layer can be shown or hidden without changing the others. Only shortcuts change the selected service types; Dotted only changes geometry choices. Timetabled buses retain actual vehicle labels and separate counts. Grey affected railways come from dated notices and do not imply bus frequency or pass eligibility. Geometry, source, pass and fee filters apply to services; closure notices remain independent. Unknown fees never qualify for No mandatory extras. Physical railways without verified service start hidden and have their own switch in Context overlays. Advanced controls contains exact timetable source selection, geometry switches and railway context. Context All/None controls all context layers; Hide all overlays clears services, railway context and stations. Supplemental bus connections without recorded pass eligibility are hidden from pass-scoped views. Controls stay closed initially and can be opened with Filters or dismissed with the close button or Escape. Station dots can follow automatic zoom thresholds, stay on at any zoom, or hide background stops. Wide views space dots out for clarity. Selected stations and the recorded endpoints of their matching routes remain visible; zooming in shows intermediate calls. Shared links retain the selections.
