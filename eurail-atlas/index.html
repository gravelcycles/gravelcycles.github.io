      Know the limits.

      This atlas represents scheduled services for 1–7 November 2026. Frequencies describe timetabled trains, not passenger use or proof of actual running. Frequency counts services between recorded station calls. Overlapping express and local paths are not summed into a single physical-track total.

      National and regional source feeds can overlap. Source filters retain that distinction; these are not certified counts of unique trains across Europe.

      The Eurail views show canonical included services plus a small, separately labelled set reviewed against current primary sources for Global Pass second class. Other conditional services appear only with the optional amber overlay; unknown eligibility stays excluded. Reservations, supplements and seat availability are separate.

      Railway alignments are source-provided or inferred from physical infrastructure. They do not certify exact operating track. Known services with unresolved geometry use dashed approximate links; these can be hidden in Filters.
