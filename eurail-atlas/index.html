      COVERAGE & SOURCES

      Explore the country coverage and source register for this dated snapshot.

      Timetable data: opentransportdata.swiss

      This atlas represents scheduled services for 1–7 November 2026. Frequencies describe timetabled trains, not passenger use or proof of actual running. Frequency counts services between recorded station calls. Overlapping express and local paths are not summed into a single physical-track total. Selecting a line adds an outline and keeps its frequency color. Where strokes overlap, clicking selects the visible stroke. A selected connection covers one source and consecutive recorded calls, not every direct train between its endpoints; use “Compare direct trains” to include intermediate stops and recorded boarding rules.

      National and regional source feeds can overlap. Source filters retain that distinction; these are not certified counts of unique trains across Europe.

      The Eurail views show canonical included services plus a small, separately labelled set reviewed against current primary sources for Global Pass second class. The optional amber dashed overlay shows conditional and unknown pass eligibility, separately from confirmed included counts. Known exclusions stay excluded. The Eurail condition filter counts only included services with explicit matching reservation or supplement rules. “No extra charge required” means no mandatory reservation or listed train supplement; optional reservations, upgrades and accommodation may cost extra. Unknown fees never qualify as free. Seat availability is not inferred.

      Railway alignments are source-provided or inferred from physical infrastructure. They do not certify exact operating track. Known services with unresolved geometry use dashed links between recorded stops. These lines can cut across land or water: they do not trace the tracks used. Click a line for an explanation, or turn off “Show approximate service alignments” in Filters. Connections with missing or invalid locations are withheld from the map; their source timetable records are retained.
